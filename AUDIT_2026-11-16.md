## 1. Summary

**Verdict: still not production-ready, about 45% (was 35%).**

The biggest change: the product's core data is now real.
- Prices, market caps, all-time highs and hourly history come from CoinGecko and Binance.
- Insights are the headlines the sentiment engines actually read.
- Themes are named by an LLM from those headlines.
- Leftover seed rows are deleted once a live source answers.
- CoinMarketCap is gone, and each RSS feed is fetched once per cycle.

Google sign-in is carefully built:
- It links accounts by Google's `sub`, not by email.
- It takes over an unverified look-alike account safely.
- It verifies the ID token's signature, audience and issuer.

Login, signup and reset are now rate-limited.

What has not moved:
- **Security and account items:** almost none of the Phase 0 list from the old roadmap was done. The JWT fallback secret, the reusable reset token, the unauthenticated pipeline refresh, the incomplete account deletion, the published demo password and `CORS *` are all still there. *Update: all six are fixed in code under Step 1 (section 9). G-05 (Atlas password rotation) was done by the owner the same day.*
- **Product honesty:** for the 12 coins the ML service doesn't cover, there are only two choices. Either no AAI at all, after `bootstrap_controller`, or made-up predictions and risk, after `seed_all`. Onboarding still throws its answers away. The auth page still shows a hard-coded "Live Markets" ticker.
- **Operations:** there is no CI and no production compose. 11 background threads plus FinBERT run inside the API process. Only 5 of 10 schedulers are safe with more than one worker, and the alert scheduler is not one of them.
