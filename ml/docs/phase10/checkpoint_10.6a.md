## 1. Where D25/D44 differ from the instructions (followed D25/D44, as instructed)
1. **D32 added.** D32 refines D25 from Phase 5 on: the top candidate must **also** beat the better baseline on the pooled ranking folds (point estimate). The 10.6a text did not mention this.
2. **D44 range rule added.** D44 also requires the range to beat the better baseline range by a paired CI of interval score. This comes on top of the coverage band.
3. **Baseline to beat.** D44 says "the strongest baseline on the confirmation folds". So "the better of B0/B1" is chosen by MdAPE on the confirmation rows.
4. **Candidate limit.** D25 allows at most 4 configs per model type and the instructions allow at most 6 configs. Both hold: 4 pooled + 2 per collection.

## 2. Target and baselines
- **Target:** y = log(price_eth / floor_point_eth), where floor_point_eth is the raw OpenSea floor point strictly before the sale (D70). Rows are clean development sales from item snapshot v3, via `load_dev()` only.
- **APE** = |exp(y_hat − y) − 1| = |fair value / price − 1|. MdAPE is pooled across collections.

**B0 floor**
- Prediction: y_hat = 0.
- Range: per collection, the 0.10 / 0.90 quantiles of the training fold's y.

**B1 rarity decile**
- Rarity score: the sum over the collection's categories of −log(share of the token's value), with "none" counted as a value. Traits only, ID-like categories (milady `Number`) excluded, over tokens with known traits (v3 `tokens.rarity_score`).
- Decile: ceil(10 × rarity_rank / tokens ranked) per collection, so decile 1 is the rarest 10%.
- Prediction: y_hat = the training-fold median y of the sale's (collection, decile), or 0 if that cell has fewer than 5 training rows.
- Range: y_hat + the per-collection 0.10 / 0.90 quantiles of the training residuals.

## 3. Features (88 model columns: 6 static + 62 trait codes + 20 dynamic)

**Notation**
- t is the sale timestamp.
- **H**, the feature history: development rows after C1–C3, without W1 rows.
  - W2-flagged rows stay in H, because W2 uses up to 30 days of look-ahead and was not known at t.
  - `clean` is never a feature.
- Every window is **[t − w, t)**, so rows at the sale's own timestamp (same transaction or block) never enter.
- premium = log(price / floor point before that sale).

| Feature | As-of definition |

## 7. Selection and bootstrap
1. Run all 6 candidates and B0/B1 on all 9 folds. Every result is reported.
2. Rank the candidates together by pooled MdAPE on folds 1–5. **Only the top-ranked candidate is confirmed.**
3. The baseline is B0 or B1, whichever has the lower MdAPE on folds 6–9 (D44).
4. **Approved** only if both of these hold:
   - the paired 95% CI of MdAPE(baseline) − MdAPE(model) on folds 6–9 excludes 0 with a positive estimate;
   - **D32:** the top candidate's ranking MdAPE is below the better baseline's ranking MdAPE.
5. **Bootstrap:** moving blocks of **7 consecutive calendar dates**. Every collection's rows of a date are resampled together, with the same date-resampling scheme as `nft_approval.date_block_ci`. 1,000 resamples, seed 0. With 116 confirmation dates that is about 16.6 blocks.

## 8. 80% range
- **Model:** LightGBM quantile models at alpha 0.10 and 0.90, using the top candidate's own structure and settings on the same training fold. This is a fixed method, so it adds no trial. Crossed bounds are sorted and counted.
- **Baselines:** empirical training-fold residual quantiles per collection (section 2).

The range is **approved** only if all of these hold on the confirmation rows:
- the point model is approved;
- coverage is in **[0.75, 0.85]**;
- each tail miss is **≤ 0.15**;
- the paired date-block CI (block 7) of mean interval score (the better baseline range minus the model) excludes 0 with a positive estimate (D44).

The bounds are in log space, so coverage is the same in price.
